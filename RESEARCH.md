# Infinity Delivery — Chilakaluripet Market Research

> A deep, in-depth research brief for launching **Infinity Delivery**, a
> groceries & daily-essentials home-delivery app, in **Chilakaluripet,
> Palnadu (ex-Guntur) district, Andhra Pradesh**.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Chilakaluripet is a fast-growing **first-grade municipality** of ~101,550
people (2011 Census), sitting on the **Vijayawada–Chilakaluripet growth
corridor** and on **National Highway 16** (Golden Quadrilateral / Asian
Highway 45). It is a **mandal headquarters** with an APMC **mandi (Tenali
Market)** at its core, a strong agricultural hinterland (rice, cotton,
maize, vegetables), and a dense cluster of kirana stores, hypermarkets and a
Reliance Smart Bazaar — but **no dominant, modern, app-first online grocery
player** that serves the town and its 15+ surrounding villages with fast,
reliable home delivery.

That gap is the opportunity. National quick-commerce players (Blinkit, Zepto,
Instamart) and bigbasket are concentrated in metro/urban-1 cities and have
limited or no meaningful presence in a Tier-3/4 town of this profile. A
**local, Telugu-first, price-competitive, farm-to-door** grocery app can win
by being *closer, cheaper, and more personal* than both the kirana and the
nationals.

**Recommendation:** Launch **Infinity Delivery** as a hyperlocal online
grocery + daily-essentials platform covering the Chilakaluripet municipality
and the 15 villages of the mandal, with a dark-store / central warehouse
near the NH16 corridor, 30–45 minute delivery inside the town, and
farm-fresh produce sourced directly from the local APMC mandi and
surrounding farmers.

## 4. Economy & Agriculture

Chilakaluripet is an **agricultural and mandi-driven economy**:

- **APMC Mandi (Tenali Market)** — the town's commercial heart. It trades
  rice, cotton, maize, pulses, and vegetables. Wholesale mandi prices are
  published daily (Agmarknet), giving us a **live price benchmark** to undercut
  retail.
- **Crops:** rice (paddy), cotton (Guntur district is AP's #1 cotton
  region), maize, pulses (chickpea, urad), groundnut, and a wide range of
  vegetables and fruits from the Krishna-basin hinterland.
- **Growth corridor:** part of the **Vijayawada–Chilakaluripet growth
  corridor**, driving urbanisation, new housing, and rising middle-class
  consumption.
- **Services:** multiple ATMs (TMB, SBI, City Union Bank, Andhra Bank),
  hospitals (Lakshmi Kids, Adithya Eye, Sivaramaiah, Lakshmi Multi Speciality),
  and colleges (Vikas College, Modern Degree College, Sadineni Chowdaraiah
  College of Arts) — indicating a **service and student economy** with
  disposable income.
- **Waste-to-energy:** a 15 MW plant project with Vijayawada & Guntur
  municipal corporations signals institutional investment in the area.

### Economic implications
- **Mandi sourcing** = a structural cost advantage: buy fresh produce at
  wholesale rates and sell at retail with healthy margin while still being
  cheaper than kirana.
- **Growth corridor** = rising urban demand for convenience, packaged goods,
  and home delivery — the exact segment an app serves.
- **Student + service population** = high-frequency, small-ticket orders
  (snacks, beverages, instant foods) that drive repeat usage.

## 5. Existing Retail & Competitive Landscape

### Local physical retail (the real competition)
- **Kirana / general stores** (dominant): Mahesh Modern Shopping Malls,
  Suraksha Stores, Subramanyeswara Avr Super Market, Sreshta Hyper Market,
  Ratna stores, Reliable Grocery Stores (Manukondavaripalem), and dozens of
  small kiranas across the 38 wards.
- **Reliance Smart Bazaar (RSM)** — Ground Floor, NRT Center, beside
  Chowdarayya Function Hall; opens 7 AM. A modern-format anchor.
- **P Mart (Chirala)** — a nearby hypermarket advertising home delivery.
- **Krishna Supermarket** — online ordering + grocery delivery (regional).

### National / quick-commerce players
- **Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart** — 10-minute quick commerce, but concentrated
  in metro & Tier-1/2 cities; **no meaningful Chilakaluripet presence**.
- **bigbasket** — online supermarket, 300+ cities, but again metro-weighted;
  limited/none in this Tier-3 town.

### Competitive gap (our wedge)
| Player | Strength | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Kirana stores | Trust, credit (udhaar), proximity | No app, no home delivery at scale, limited range, no offers |
| Reliance Smart Bazaar | Range, pricing | Store-only, no fast home delivery, impersonal |
| P Mart / Krishna | Some delivery | Regional, not local, limited local sourcing |
| Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart | Speed, tech | **Not present** in Chilakaluripet; metro pricing |
| bigbasket | Range | Not present; metro pricing |

**Infinity Delivery's position:** *"Your local mandi, delivered to your door
in 30 minutes — in Telugu, at the best price."* We combine the **trust of the
kirana**, the **range of the hypermarket**, and the **convenience of the
app**, at **mandi-competitive prices**.

## 6. Target Segments

1. **Urban household managers** (municipality, 38 wards) — daily grocery,
   fresh produce, dairy, packaged staples. *Primary.*
2. **Mandal village households** (15 villages: Purushottapatnam,
   Manukondavaripalem, Pasumarru, Ganapavaram, Boppudi, etc.) — weekly
   grocery, staples, bulk rice/oil. *High growth.*
3. **Students & young professionals** (colleges, service jobs) — snacks,
   beverages, instant foods, late-night orders. *High frequency.*
4. **Small businesses / canteens / function halls** — bulk and recurring
   orders (B2B). *Margin & volume.*
5. **Health-conscious & elderly** — organic/local produce, medicines-adjacent
   daily needs, assisted phone ordering. *Loyalty.*

## 8. Operations & Logistics Model

- **Dark store / central warehouse** near the NH16 corridor or NRT Center
  (high footfall, central).
- **Sourcing:** daily pre-dawn procurement at the APMC mandi + contracts with
  5–10 surrounding farms for guaranteed fresh produce.
- **Delivery fleet:** 8–12 bikes initially (town) + 2 tempos for village
  bulk; 30–45 min SLA inside the municipality, same-day for villages.
- **Zoning:** 38 wards + 15 villages mapped into delivery zones with
  per-zone SLAs and pin-code coverage (522616 core).
- **Payments:** UPI (dominant in AP), cards, wallet, and **cash on
  delivery** (still important in Tier-3).
- **Support:** Telugu WhatsApp + phone ordering for non-app users.

---

## 9. Go-to-Market & Pricing

- **Launch offer:** free delivery on first order; ₹50 off on orders above
  ₹299.
- **Subscription:** "Infinity Plus" — free delivery + member pricing (₹99/mo).
- **Referral:** ₹30 for both referee and refereee.
- **Channels:** app + web + WhatsApp Business + local radio + temple/market
  footfall + college tie-ups.
- **Pricing rule:** ≤ kirana on staples, ≤ RSM on packaged goods, mandi-anchored
  on fresh produce.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Kirana credit (udhaar) habit | Offer a small "buy now, pay in 7 days" wallet for loyal users |
| Fresh-produce wastage | Mandi-anchored daily buying, dynamic discounting near EOD |
| National players entering | Win on locality, Telugu UX, mandi pricing, and speed |
| Delivery cost in villages | Batched same-day village runs; minimum order for villages |
| Low smartphone penetration in villages | WhatsApp/phone ordering + COD |
